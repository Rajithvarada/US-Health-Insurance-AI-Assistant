\# Phase 1 – Source Collection Report



\## 1. Objective



The objective of Phase 1 was to identify and organize reliable government sources containing information about US health insurance, healthcare coverage, consumer rights, Medicare, Medicaid, and related topics.



\## 2. Source Registry



A source registry was created to maintain the collected source information in a structured format.



\*\*File:\*\* `data/raw/source\_registry.csv`



\*\*Total registered sources:\*\* 215



The registry contains the following fields:



| Field | Description |

|---|---|

| source\_id | Unique source identifier |

| source\_name | Name of the source |

| source\_type | Type of government source |

| source\_url | Source webpage or document URL |

| topic | Related health insurance topic |



\## 3. Source Type Distribution



| Source Type | Count |

|---|---:|

| Government Website | 204 |

| Government PDF | 10 |

| Government Publication | 1 |

| \*\*Total\*\* | \*\*215\*\* |



All entries in the registry are categorized as government websites, government PDFs, or government publications.



\## 4. Example Sources



The registry includes HealthCare.gov resources such as:



\- HealthCare.gov Glossary

\- HealthCare.gov Deductible

\- HealthCare.gov Copayment



These resources cover general health insurance terminology and specific insurance concepts.



\## 5. Topics Covered



The source registry covers multiple US health insurance subject areas, including:



\- General health insurance terminology

\- Deductibles, copayments, and coinsurance

\- Health Insurance Marketplace

\- Medicare and Medicaid

\- Prescription drug coverage

\- Employer-sponsored health plans

\- Appeals and consumer protection

\- Preventive healthcare

\- Provider networks

\- Health Savings Accounts



\## 6. Output



The primary output of this phase was:



`data/raw/source\_registry.csv`



The registry contains 215 source entries and provides the source information used in the subsequent raw dataset collection phase.



\## 7. Conclusion



Phase 1 – Source Collection was completed with 215 registered government source entries. The structured source registry provided the foundation for collecting raw US health insurance information in Phase 2.
